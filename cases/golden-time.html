01 / The Opportunity

Companionship Without Stigma.

GoldenTime was born from a mission to address a critical issue facing older adults: daily loneliness. It is a shared-experience social platform where seniors can create or join local leisure activities—from a casual coffee meeting to a neighborhood walk or museum visit.

It is intentionally not a dating app; it makes low-pressure, platonic companionship feel safe, local, and possible.

Core Objective: Build an onboarding journey that balances rigorous identity verification with zero technical friction for older users.

Design Intent

Social Connection vs. Dating Friction

❌ Dating App Patterns

High romantic pressure, superficial swiping, and awkward social expectations.

✓ GoldenTime Shared Activities

Low-pressure, interest-based gatherings anchored in local community safety.

02 / Research Insights

The 4 Core Challenges.

Understanding the emotional, psychological, and physical barriers facing older adults online.

🛡️

01. The Safety Gap

For seniors, digital safety is not a feature; it is a dealbreaker. Identity verification needed to earn trust before the first social meetup without feeling invasive.

⚡

02. Eliminating Error-Phobia

Older adults frequently fear that one wrong tap will permanently break something. Registration had to feel like a warm, conversational guide, not a technical test.

👁️

03. Radically Inclusive UI

Age-related visual and motor changes make standard mobile elements frustrating. Oversized hit areas and high-contrast typography are foundational requirements.

☕

04. Intent-Driven Positioning

Seniors seek connection while avoiding dating stigma. The product language strictly emphasizes shared daytime activities and companionship.

03 / User Persona

Dalia Bar Lev (70).

“I miss doing things with friends. At my age, I’m left to deal with everything alone.”

Background: A retired nurse living in Givatayim. Active and social, but her circle has shrunk as peers become busy with grandkids or physical limitations.

  • The Tech Gap: Uses WhatsApp and Waze daily, but fears making irreversible mistakes when exploring unfamiliar applications.
  • The Trust Need: Will not meet anyone unless 100% confident their identity has been verified by the system.
  • Accessibility Need: Generous touch targets and large typography that don't require changing reading glasses every two minutes.

04 / High-Trust Interaction

Bridging the Trust Gap.

The Paradox: Seniors demand strict identity verification to feel safe, yet taking selfies and submitting documents causes intense technical intimidation.

Rather than presenting verification as a bureaucratic chore, GoldenTime frames it as a Mutual Safety Shield. Conversational, single-task screens and persistent progress indicators remove fear of the unknown.

Result: Turns a stressful camera selfie into a protected, empowering milestone.

The 4-Stage Safety Shield

Verification Stages

01. A Warm Welcome: Setting safety expectations in conversational language.
02. Basic Details: One focused task at a time with roomy touch targets.
03. Safety Selfie: Clear visual framing turning a selfie into a mutual shield.
04. Verified Profile: Immediate visible gold star reward badge.

05 / The Experience

Onboarding, Step by Step.

How each screen was designed to guide, protect, and reward older adults.

👋

Step 01 / Reassure

A Warm Welcome: Establishes GoldenTime as a trusted community and explains why safety verification protects everyone. No jargon, no rush.

📝

Step 02 / Guide

One Small Task at a Time: Chunked fields and prominent progress indicators lower cognitive load. The UI gives context, then gets out of the way.

🛡️

Step 03 / Protect

Mutual Safety Selfie: Framed as a collective shield rather than an ID check. Forgiving instructions and large camera controls ease friction.

⭐

Step 04 / Reward

Show Trust: Dalia receives a verified gold badge—a clear, instant social signal that she and everyone she meets took the exact same safety steps.

06 / Outcomes & Reflection

Impact & Key Takeaways.

How empathetic research created a safe, joyful digital gateway for older adults.

🎯

Trust as an Interaction Pattern

Safety is not built through legal disclaimers; it is constructed through conversational tone, clear feedback, and visible social verification signals.

🤝

Accessible Design is Universal Design

Designing for seniors—with large touch targets, single-task screens, and high contrast—creates a cleaner, better experience for users of all ages.

💡

Overcoming Error-Phobia

Providing forgiving, reversible navigation and friendly guidance successfully dissolved the fear of making permanent mistakes.
