01 / The opportunity

Overview.

GoldenTime was born from a mission to address a critical issue facing older adults: daily loneliness. It is a shared-experience social platform where people can create or join local leisure activities — from a simple coffee meeting to a walk or museum visit. It is intentionally not a dating app; it makes low-pressure companionship feel safe, local, and possible.

02 / Research insights

The Challenges.

  1. 01

    The Safety
    Gap

    For seniors, digital safety is not a feature; it is a dealbreaker. Identity verification needed to earn trust before the first social interaction, without feeling invasive.

  2. 02

    Eliminating
    Error-Phobia

    Many older adults worry that one wrong click will break something permanently. Registration had to feel like a reassuring conversation, not a technical test.

  3. 03

    Radically
    Inclusive UI

    Age-related sensory and motor changes can make standard mobile patterns frustrating. Oversized hit areas and high-contrast typography became foundational, not decorative.

  4. 04

    Intent-Driven
    Positioning

    People may want connection but avoid the stigma of dating products. The experience needed language and cues that clearly centered shared activities and companionship.

04 / High-trust interaction

Bridging the Trust Gap:
The Verification Flow.

The problem

The Safety vs.
Complexity Paradox

  • Identity trustDalia worries about impersonators and has no reason to trust an unfamiliar profile by default.
  • Technological intimidationShe fears a wrong tap could cause irreversible damage or expose private information.
  • Selfie frictionTaking a selfie feels bothersome and technically challenging, even when she views it as the price of personal safety.

The solution

A Guided
Safety Shield

Rather than presenting verification as a formality, the journey frames it as a visible layer of protection. Conversational, chunked onboarding makes every step comprehensible, and a persistent progress indicator removes uncertainty about what comes next.

05 / The experience

Onboarding, step by step.

Step 01 / Reassure

A warm, clear welcome

The first screen establishes GoldenTime as a shared-experience space and describes why a few simple steps make every member safer. No jargon, no urgency, and no tiny actions.

Step 02 / Guide

One small task at a time

Chunked fields and obvious progress feedback lower cognitive load. The interface gives enough context to act, then gets out of the way.

Step 03 / Protect

Make the selfie feel worth it

Instead of a cold identity check, the safety selfie is framed as a mutual shield. Clear instructions, forgiving language, and generous controls reduce friction.

Step 04 / Reward

Show trust, don’t hide it

The successful state gives Dalia a verified badge she can understand at a glance — tangible reassurance for herself and everyone she meets.
