The Methodology

How I Crack Instructional Problems.

A transparent 4-stage framework for turning complex subject matter into high-impact learning products.

01 Cognitive Gap & Audience Discovery
▼

I start by diagnosing the exact cognitive bottleneck: Is it a knowledge gap (they don't know the rules), a fluency gap (they know the rules but hesitate under pressure), or a motivation gap? I map the audience's mental models and identify the high-risk failure points in their actual working environment.

02 Experiential Metaphors & Interaction Loops
▼

Rather than defaulting to generic bullet points or multiple-choice questions, I find an experiential anchor (e.g., an airport conveyor belt for categorical sorting, a dinner-rush table for restaurant service). Every mechanic is engineered to reinforce the target cognitive pathway and sustain flow state.

03 Rapid Prototyping in Unity, Storyline & Web
▼

I turn instructional storyboards into functional code and interactive prototypes quickly. Handling both design and programming ensures that pedagogical subtleties—such as non-punitive mastery loops and pause occlusion—are preserved accurately in the final build.

04 AI Acceleration & Pedagogical Guardrails
▼

I deploy generative AI to eliminate tedious manual authoring while establishing strict defensive guardrails. The result is a scalable educational system where instructors can produce high-quality customized content in minutes.
