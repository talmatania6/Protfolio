problem:
  eyebrow: "01 / The Problem"
  heading: "The Onboarding Reality"
  context: >-
    After working at the restaurant for two years, I repeatedly saw the same onboarding challenge: new waiters were expected to learn a detailed service process while already performing it during live shifts.
  body: >-
    The restaurant maintains a high and consistent service standard, but many new waiters begin with limited or no previous restaurant experience. This creates inconsistencies in service, ordering mistakes, difficulty maintaining the meal flow, and additional pressure on experienced staff who need to correct mistakes in real time.

challenge:
  eyebrow: "02 / The Challenge"
  heading: "The Challenge"
  body: >-
    The instructional challenge was to move beyond teaching service rules. New waiters needed to recognize the correct sequence, make decisions in context, prioritize competing tasks, and apply the restaurant’s standards confidently during busy service.
  takeaway: "The problem was not simply a lack of information. Learners needed to perform the service process correctly in real situations."
  problems:
    - Follow the full service sequence correctly.
    - Apply ordering and service procedures in context.
    - Maintain prioritization, accuracy and professionalism under pressure.

learningSolution:
  eyebrow: "03 / The Learning Solution"
  heading: "Designing Training for a Digital-First Audience"
  context: >-
    Most new waiters are between 17 and 23 and are highly familiar with digital, visual and interactive experiences. Instead of relying on a long service manual or passive presentation, I chose to create a short interactive onboarding experience that mirrors the environment in which they actually work.
  body: >-
    The module prepares learners for their first shifts by placing restaurant procedures inside realistic situations, decisions and practice activities.

contentStrategy:
  eyebrow: "04 / Content Strategy"
  heading: "Turning the Service Process into a Learning Journey"
  body: >-
    I broke the restaurant’s service standards into the real sequence a waiter experiences during a shift. Instead of organizing the module as disconnected topics, the content follows the service journey from opening a table through closing the guest experience.
  architecture: >-
    The Service Cycle became the learning architecture of the module. Each stage represents a chapter, and learners return to the cycle as they progress through the experience.
  decisions:
    - title: Chronological Structure
      body: The content follows the real service flow, allowing learners to build their understanding in the same order they will use it on the floor.
    - title: Learn → Practice
      body: Key procedures are first demonstrated or explained and then immediately reinforced through an interaction or decision.
    - title: Progressive Complexity
      body: Learners begin with individual procedures and gradually move toward prioritization and decision-making during busy service.

experience:
  eyebrow: "05 / Learning Experience"
  heading: "From Instruction to Practice"
  interactiveVideo:
    heading: Interactive Video Scenarios
    body: Real restaurant footage pauses at key moments, requiring learners to make decisions before the scenario continues.
  learnPractice:
    heading: Learn → Practice
    body: Service procedures are first broken into clear steps and then reinforced through active practice.
  knowledgeCheck:
    heading: Scenario-Based Decisions
    body: Realistic workplace situations turn knowledge checks into contextual decision-making rather than simple recall.
  prioritization:
    heading: Prioritization Under Pressure
    body: As complexity increases, learners practice deciding which guest needs should be handled first during a busy service.
  progression:
    heading: Progressive Learning Path
    body: After completing a chapter, learners return to the Service Cycle, see their progress and unlock the next stage.

assessment:
  eyebrow: "06 / Assessment"
  heading: "From Practice to Certification"
  body: >-
    After completing the full Service Cycle, learners unlock a final certification assessment designed to check their understanding of the restaurant’s core service standards.
