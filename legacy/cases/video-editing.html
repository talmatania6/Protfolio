01 / The Brief

Performance Over Tool Menus.

The challenge was not to explain video-editing tools, but to help beginners understand what to do, in what order, and why each editorial decision matters.

I began by defining the target performance: enabling a learner to plan, execute, and export a publishable first video with confidence. I identified the minimum essential knowledge, eliminated advanced feature bloat, and mapped the remainder into a clean visual journey.

Instructional Principle: Remove software feature-overload. Focus entirely on the immediate goal of producing a publishable first video.

Instructional Reframing

Software Manual vs. Performance Path

❌ Software Menu Approach

Explaining 50+ toolbars and settings before the learner even touches footage.

✓ Goal-Oriented Creator Flow

Step-by-step task execution answering: "What do I cut first and why?"

02 / Audience Insights

Designing for Beginner Creators.

The audience is digitally native, but not yet fluent in editing. They are motivated by publishing a stronger video—not by learning software.

🎯

Outcome-Driven

They want the fastest direct route to a visible, polished result they can proudly share on social media.

🛡️

Overcoming Low Confidence

Complex interfaces and technical jargon cause analysis paralysis; clear scaffolding restores momentum.

⏱️

Short Learning Moments

They learn by doing in short bursts. Content must support quick on-demand references while editing.

📱

Platform-Agnostic Principles

Principles (pacing, cuts, lighting) transfer seamlessly across TikTok, YouTube, and Reels.

04 / Learning Strategy

Why a One-Page Website?.

A responsive one-page website minimizes navigation friction, preserves the chronological learning sequence, and allows creators to keep the guide open on a secondary screen while editing.

  • Performance-Centered Architecture: Organizes around creating the video, not software toolbars.
  • Chunking Without Context Loss: Interactive tabs and modals deliver depth on demand without scattering navigation.
  • Worked Examples: Deconstructs real creator videos to make abstract pacing principles immediately observable.

Design Win: Frictionless dual-screen reference preserving the learner's creative momentum.

Connected Learning Ecosystem

5 Core Page Stages

01. Foundations: The 4 core principles (Cut, Light, Text, Sound).
02. Production Flow: 7-step roadmap from raw clips to final export.
03. Personalization: Interactive diagnostic quiz matching style.
04. Worked Examples: Story-based creator inspiration gallery.
05. FAQ Support: On-demand troubleshooting accordions.

05 / Instructional UX/UI

From Logic to Layout.

Each interface pattern was born from an instructional requirement: what does the learner need to decide or do next?

🚀

01. Hero Promise ("Make The Cut")

Need: Establish value before software intimidation.
Decision: Frame around creating a publishable video with a clear guided path.

📑

02. Tabbed Principles (Cut / Light / Text / Sound)

Need: Foundational mental models.
Decision: Chunk 4 universal pillars into interactive tabs, reducing simultaneous cognitive load.

🛤️

03. The 7-Stage Process Line

Need: Build end-to-end task awareness.
Decision: Persistent timeline showing current stage, with modals for on-demand details.

🎨

04. Personalization Quiz ("What's Your Vibe?")

Need: Tailor principles to learner platform.
Decision: 3-question diagnostic providing custom tool recommendations.

👁️

05. Inspiration Worked Gallery

Need: Bridge theory to actual video formats.
Decision: Social-story format deconstructing techniques behind real viral edits.

❓

06. Performance Support FAQ

Need: Fast troubleshooting without clutter.
Decision: Clean accordion keeping primary learning path lean.
